⚡ Live editor

Tap or click an element to select it, then drag to move it. Drag the white circle to resize (Shift = keep proportions). To replace a photo, select it and press Replace image in the edit panel. Arrow keys = nudge, Delete = remove. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in or out; when zoomed, drag the empty space to move around. Drag the purple ↻ circle above an element to rotate it (snaps every 15°). On a phone, double-tap an element to open its edit panel at the bottom; press ✓ Done to close it.
